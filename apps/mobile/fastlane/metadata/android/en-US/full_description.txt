ARIS (Animal Resources Information System) is the official mobile application of the African Union Inter-African Bureau for Animal Resources (AU-IBAR).

ARIS enables field agents, veterinary officers, and national administrators across 55 African Member States to:

- Collect and submit animal health surveillance data, even offline
- Monitor disease outbreaks with real-time alerts and push notifications
- Track vaccination campaigns and field activities
- Access continental dashboards with KPIs across 9 domains
- Navigate offline maps with GPS tracking for field operations
- Submit data through customizable form templates
- Synchronize data automatically when connectivity is restored

Covering 9 business domains:
1. Animal Health & One Health
2. Livestock Production & Pastoralism
3. Fisheries & Aquaculture
4. Trade, Markets & SPS
5. Wildlife & Biodiversity
6. Apiculture & Pollination
7. Climate & Environment
8. Governance & Capacities
9. Knowledge Management

Key features:
- Offline-first architecture with automatic sync
- Multi-language support (English, French, Portuguese, Arabic)
- Biometric authentication and PIN lock
- GPS tracking for transhumance corridors and field activities
- Photo capture with compression and metadata
- Role-based access control (8 roles)
- Multi-tenant support (Continental, REC, Member State levels)
- Push notifications for outbreak alerts

ARIS is part of the AU Digital Transformation Strategy 2020-2030, aligned with Agenda 2063 and the Livestock Development Strategy for Africa (LiDeSA).

Developed by AU-IBAR, Nairobi, Kenya.
